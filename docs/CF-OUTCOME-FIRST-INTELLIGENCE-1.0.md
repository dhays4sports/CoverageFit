**Status:** Documentation-only proposed Roadmap 3 operating lens. Not a release or live dogfood approval.

## Customer outcome, not a generic CRM

- **Agency / producer:** convert available, properly permissioned lead evidence into **incremental useful conversations, suitable quotes and producer-verified bound business** per producer-hour, with spend, response quality and attribution captured separately.
- **Consumer:** gain understandable, evidence-grounded protection insight and a helpful next action over a persistent relationship. Retain the existing `COVERAGEFIT_NORTH_STAR.md` consumer-first direction; producer acquisition is a near-term proof, not a replacement North Star.
- **408FARMERS:** remains the acquisition and consented handoff surface; AgencyZoom remains cadence/CRM owner; RingCentral/provider capabilities are not silently replaced.

## Minimum proof before expansion

`Import / acquisition evidence -> canonical SIGNAL interpretation -> appropriate producer attention -> consent-appropriate action -> verified human outcome -> feedback/measurement.`

Use a small, authorized, properly isolated synthetic-to-pilot progression. Capture source attribution, meaningful contact, quote delivered, explicitly confirmed bind, producer minutes, provider spend, missed/incorrect decisions, and withheld/missing denominators. No raw reply, queue rank, draft send or inferred premium is a customer win.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

No customer-level data inspection for this doctrine. No protected/demographic proxy ranking, permission inferred from anonymous SIGNAL, or consumer exposure of internal priority dimensions. Exact proposals/quotes and insurance binding require applicable licensed and carrier-authorized review. No conflation of engagement with conversions. Preview tests and passing CI are not proof of production correctness. Keep all existing Roadmap 3 draft PRs, Cloudflare isolation review, rate-limit behavior investigation, and release approvals separate and unchanged.

## Required roadmap review fields

For every substantive addition: customer job and payer; specified outcome; observable baseline/evidence; producer effort and costs; consent and authority; provider/CRM ownership; reuse-vs-build; understandable consumer and producer UX; falsification threshold; explicit readiness level; next smallest reversible experiment. If unknown, write **UNKNOWN**, not a confidence claim.
